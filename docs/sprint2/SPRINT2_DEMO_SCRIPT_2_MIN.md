## Recording checklist

- Use the exact release-candidate commit; record its SHA in the video description or accompanying notes.
- Use synthetic accounts, sample internships, and dummy resumes only.
- Show a real running browser session; avoid slides-only or mockup-only footage.
- Keep the recording to approximately 2 minutes and ensure text is readable.
- Do not show `.env`, passwords, tokens, private email addresses, real student records, or database credentials.
- Upload the video to the repository as required by the course deliverables and add its repository path/link to the Sprint 2 report.
